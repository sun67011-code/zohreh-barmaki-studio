Creative philosophy

Every cut should
serve the story.

From a music performance to a specialist brand introduction, moving images need purposeful pacing, meaningful composition and a consistent visual language. I combine editing, design judgment and delivery planning to create film assets suited to their actual use.

Creative directions

Ideas, form
and expression.

These directions can be shaped into an original project based on the story, material, audience and intended format.

01 / DIRECTION

Cinematic teaser & promotional film

Concept treatment, selected moments, pacing, transitions, typography and music-led editing.

A concise film that communicates mood and identity.
02 / DIRECTION

Short-form social editing

Reframe longer footage into vertical reels, stories and brief platform-specific cuts.

A more focused viewing experience across channels.
03 / DIRECTION

Performance & artist videos

Edit concerts, rehearsals, interviews, installations and mixed-media sequences while respecting the work.

Professional presentation of performances and creative material.
04 / DIRECTION

Technical finishing & delivery

Image cleanup, aspect-ratio adaptation, captions or title frames, and suitable high-quality exports for web or presentations.

Useful, compatible versions for their destination.
