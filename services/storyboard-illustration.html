Creative philosophy

Give narrative a
visual structure.

Stories need decisions about rhythm, viewpoint, characters, composition and atmosphere. I work across storyboard planning and illustration concepts to make the narrative more precise, expressive and visually consistent.

Creative directions

Ideas, form
and expression.

These directions can be shaped into an original project based on the story, material, audience and intended format.

01 / DIRECTION

Film & teaser storyboards

Scene-by-scene visual frames, shot progression and narrative timing guidance.

A workable visual plan before production.
02 / DIRECTION

Book and novel illustration

Concept sketches and artwork directions for chapters, covers, illustrated sequences and editorial passages.

Illustration that supports the voice of the text.
03 / DIRECTION

Character & environment exploration

Visual research, silhouette, mood, palette and contextual studies for people and places.

A consistent visual world rather than disconnected images.
04 / DIRECTION

Sequential visual narrative

Panels, composition, pacing and transitions for comics, picture books or visual essays.

Clarity from one frame or page to the next.
