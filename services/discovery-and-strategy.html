01Independent practice / ZB

The approach

Clarity before code.

The most expensive problem in a digital project is often an unexamined assumption. I connect stakeholder goals, real user behavior, process dependencies and technical constraints before the build begins.

What we can build

Purpose-built,
not one-size-fits-all.

Potential workstreams shaped around your use case. Final scope is defined after discovery, not assumed before it.

01 / FOCUS

Product discovery & definition

Clarify target audiences, goals, current systems, user pains and success criteria.

A focused project definition with decision-ready priorities.
02 / FOCUS

Information & interaction architecture

Map information hierarchy, primary journeys, navigation, access and operational states.

A coherent foundation for design and engineering.
03 / FOCUS

Technical feasibility & integration planning

Evaluate system boundaries, hosting approaches, API needs, maintainability and delivery risks.

A realistic implementation path, without premature platform commitment.
04 / FOCUS

MVP planning & phased delivery

Separate essential workflows from later enhancements and structure deliverables into meaningful releases.

A launchable first stage that can evolve.

Outputs & deliverables

Clear work.
Tangible outcomes.

Deliverables depend on scope and stage; the following are examples of what a focused engagement may include.

  1. 01A concise requirements and scope document
  2. 02User roles, workflows and prioritized journeys
  3. 03Site or system architecture and integration map
  4. 04A phased roadmap with technical assumptions
  5. 05A handover-ready brief for design and engineering

Workflow

A considered process,
from first brief to handover.

Each stage has a clear decision point so scope and expectations remain visible.

  1. 01Discover

    Goals, audience, source material, operations and constraints.

  2. 02Design

    Direction, requirements, experience and draft deliverables.

  3. 03Develop

    Iterate through implementation and practical review.

  4. 04Deliver

    Finalize, document and prepare for the next stage.
