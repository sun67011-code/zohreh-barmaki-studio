Privacy

Simple by
design.

This website is a professional portfolio and project-enquiry experience hosted on GitHub Pages, with a secure Supabase backend used only when you choose to submit a project enquiry.

Project-enquiry information is transmitted only when you explicitly submit the form. The submitted business and contact information is stored for review and follow-up.

For healthcare enquiries, do not enter patient-identifiable health information, clinical records, passwords, API keys or other confidential credentials into the public form.

The site may load externally hosted font files and link to third-party services such as GitHub, Instagram, LinkedIn and live project websites. Those services apply their own privacy practices when you visit them.

The enquiry backend may retain limited technical metadata needed for abuse prevention. The private client platform uses authenticated access for project records, milestones, updates, proposals, documents and project communication. No advertising trackers are intentionally installed.
